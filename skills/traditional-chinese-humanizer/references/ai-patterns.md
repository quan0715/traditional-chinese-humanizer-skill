# AI writing patterns

Use this reference when a draft is long, public-facing, high-stakes, or visibly AI-generated. Apply judgment; do not mechanically replace every phrase if the sentence already carries precise meaning.

## Empty significance

Watch for claims that inflate importance without evidence:

- `標誌著...關鍵時刻`
- `彰顯...重要性`
- `反映更廣泛的趨勢`
- `深深植根於`
- `不可磨滅的印記`
- `持續演變的格局`

Rewrite by naming the actual event, role, evidence, or consequence.

Before:

> 這次更新標誌著產品創新旅程中的重要里程碑，彰顯團隊對未來工作型態的承諾。

After:

> 這次更新新增批次匯入、權限分層與離線草稿，讓管理者可以先整理資料，再分批開放給團隊使用。

## Promotional language

Watch for:

- `無縫`, `全方位`, `前所未有`, `突破性`, `沉浸式`
- `打造`, `賦能`, `開啟新篇章`, `重新定義`
- `更智慧`, `更有溫度`, `極致體驗`

Rewrite with product behavior, workflow effect, or measurable outcome.

## Vague attribution

Avoid vague authorities unless the source is named:

- `業界專家指出`
- `多份報告顯示`
- `外界普遍認為`
- `有觀察者表示`

Use a named source, a supplied document, or remove the sentence.

## Chatbot residue

Remove conversation scaffolding when it appears inside deliverable text:

- `當然可以`
- `以下是...`
- `希望這對你有幫助`
- `如果你需要，我也可以...`
- `你說得完全正確`

Rewrite as the actual content.

## Passive packaging

High-risk forms:

- `讓資料可以被使用`
- `讓問題被看見`
- `內容能被有效管理`
- `這些資訊將被整合`

Rewrite with an actor:

- `系統整理資料，供教師匯出成班級報表`
- `後台標示逾期項目，讓管理者先處理高風險案件`

## Mechanical structure

Watch for:

- every paragraph ending with a short "therefore" sentence,
- every list item starting with bold labels and a colon,
- repeated three-part lists,
- repeated `此外`, `然而`, `值得注意的是`,
- overuse of em dashes or decorative punctuation.

Rewrite by combining list items, changing sentence length, or deleting transitions that do not change meaning.

## Generic conclusions

Avoid endings like:

- `未來值得期待`
- `邁向更美好的明天`
- `這只是開始`
- `持續追求卓越`

End with a concrete next step, limitation, result, or open question.

## Human voice without sloppiness

Human writing can have a view. It can admit uncertainty, choose a sharper verb, or use a short sentence after a dense one. Keep that voice grounded in the material. Do not add jokes, emotional claims, or first-person phrasing unless the genre supports it.
